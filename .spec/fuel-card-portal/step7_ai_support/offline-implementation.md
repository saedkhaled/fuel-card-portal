# Package 07 · Offline implementation

The initial center Support demo used five sample Arabic answers for balances, stations, top-up, invoices, and prices. It supports Arabic and Syrian phrasing and selected English aliases, with an Arabic reply and a link to the relevant portal screen. Unmatched or ambiguous questions offer the existing FAQ. Chat history and drafts are scoped to the current sign-in. Balances is reachable from Home and More.

The initial demo deferred the customer guide and connected answering. The [current offline design](package.md#offline-support-design) and the [Gemini pilot plan](gemini-pilot.md) describe the latest scope. Gemini Developer API Free is the pilot candidate and Cloudflare Workers AI is the alternative for evaluation against the same Syrian Arabic questions. Google currently lists Syria as supported and may use free-tier requests and responses to improve its products. Cloudflare publishes a daily free allowance and says it does not train on Workers AI customer prompts or answers. [Gemini availability](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/available-regions) · [Gemini free-tier terms](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms#unpaid-services) · [Workers AI pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/) · [Workers AI data usage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/)
